# Story Bible — *The Ledger of Ash & Iron*

> **Single Source of Truth** for characters, world rules, locations, tone, and 10-chapter narrative structure.
> Note: Where Section 4 was referenced externally, consistent low-fantasy guild-chronicle lore has been established below and all assumptions are logged in `docs/PROGRESS.md`.

## 1. World & Tone

- **Genre**: Grounded, low-fantasy political & human drama (no magic, no glowing supernatural effects, no monsters). Conflict comes from scarcity, broken supply lines, guild politics, drought, war profiteering, and personal loyalties.
- **Premise**: After two dry harvests and a winter border skirmish, the regional Great Guild issues an emergency iron-and-grain requisition. **Torrik**, an apprentice clerk from a modest outer guild hall, is dispatched with a sealed dispatch ledger to audit frontier posts—only to uncover falsified quotas, diverted river barges, abandoned villages, and a fractured guild council.
- **Visual Rules**:
  - Clean, stylized flat-vector anime look with consistent outlines.
  - All outfits are fully modest; no exposed skin beyond face and hands.
  - No magic or glowing supernatural particles—only natural rain, dust, and forge/camp embers.

## 2. Cast of Characters (9 Characters)

1. **Torrik** (`torrik`)
   - **Role**: Junior Guild Chronicler & Protagonist
   - **Signature Accessory**: Bound field notebook
   - **Appearance**: Warm olive-tan skin, dark chestnut cropped hair, warm amber-brown eyes, practical slate-indigo tunic with brass clasp.
   - **Personality**: Observant, methodical, cautious under pressure, writes down discrepancies others ignore.

2. **Orsen** (`orsen`)
   - **Role**: Master Blacksmith of the Outer Ward
   - **Signature Accessory**: Heavy soot-stained leather apron
   - **Appearance**: Sun-weathered bronze skin, close-cropped charcoal hair, steady steel-grey eyes, rolled-sleeve linen shirt beneath his thick leather apron.
   - **Personality**: Blunt, fiercely protective of his apprentices, distrusts Great Guild decrees when iron quotas rise without coal shipments.

3. **Myra** (`myra`)
   - **Role**: Road Courier & Reconnaissance Scout
   - **Signature Accessory**: Cross-body leather courier satchel and travel hood
   - **Appearance**: Fair-warm skin with wind-freckled cheeks, auburn braided hair tucked under a travel hood, sharp moss-green eyes, layered forest-drab travel cloak and tunic.
   - **Personality**: Quick-witted, pragmatic, knows every back road and muddy shortcut between the city and the border camp.

4. **Haldor** (`haldor`)
   - **Role**: Veteran Commander of the Small Guild Hall
   - **Signature Accessory**: Full grey beard and heavy weathered shoulder cape
   - **Appearance**: Pale weathered skin, silver-grey swept hair and trimmed grey beard, pale slate-blue eyes, charcoal wool coat with a dark crimson-lined cape.
   - **Personality**: Stoic, weary, bound by old oaths; tries to shield Torrik from council politics while knowing the truth cannot stay buried.

5. **Nova** (`nova`)
   - **Role**: Quiet Wayfarer & Unofficial Witness
   - **Signature Accessory**: Oversized draped travel cloak
   - **Appearance**: Cool pale-ivory skin, straight midnight-indigo hair framing the face, pale silver-violet eyes, oversized slate-plum cloak covering high-collared modest layers.
   - **Personality**: Speaks sparingly, notices what crowds overlook, carries messages for displaced farmers rather than guild banners.

6. **Fennick** (`fennick`)
   - **Role**: Great Guild Quartermaster & Envoy
   - **Signature Accessory**: Flashy gold-trimmed crimson cloak
   - **Appearance**: Warm golden-tan skin, neatly styled sandy-blonde hair, bright hazel-gold eyes, tailored cream-and-plum doublet beneath a flashy crimson cloak with gold piping.
   - **Personality**: Smooth-talking, theatrical, treats human hardship as an accounting problem; always smiling until cornered by hard numbers.

7. **Ilvar** (`ilvar`)
   - **Role**: Senior Guild Archivist & Investigator
   - **Signature Accessory**: High-collared dark long coat
   - **Appearance**: Cool olive-pale skin, silver-streaked dark ash hair, sharp dark teal eyes, buttoned charcoal-navy long coat with brass fasteners.
   - **Personality**: Disciplined, unsentimental, keeps duplicate ledgers locked behind oak doors; respects precision over rank.

8. **Selva** (`selva`) *(Sister to Rowan)*
   - **Role**: Displaced Villager & Relief Organizer
   - **Signature Accessory**: Woven ochre-and-indigo headscarf
   - **Appearance**: **Warm terracotta-tan skin** and **deep warm copper-hazel eyes** (shared identically with Rowan to clearly read as family), dark umber hair tucked neatly under her headscarf, modest layered earth-toned dress and shawl.
   - **Personality**: Resilient, steady, refuses to let her ruined village become a mere footnote in a council ledger.

9. **Rowan** (`rowan`) *(Brother to Selva)*
   - **Role**: Conscripted Militia Shield-Bearer
   - **Signature Accessory**: Pierced iron-rimmed wooden shield and gaunt, hollow-cheeked face
   - **Appearance**: **Warm terracotta-tan skin** and **deep warm copper-hazel eyes** (shared identically with Selva), tousled dark umber hair, gaunt facial shading, patched wool gambeson and modest leather bracers.
   - **Personality**: Exhausted, fiercely loyal to his sister Selva, cynical toward guild officers after holding the mountain road through freezing rain.

## 5. Ten-Chapter Narrative Arc

1. **Chapter 1: Iron & Ink** (`small_guild_hall`, `blacksmith_workshop`, `city_square`, `rainy_street`)
   - Haldor gives Torrik the sealed audit. Orsen reveals missing coal carts. Fennick brushes the discrepancy aside in the square, and Myra rides in through the rain with word that the border camp received no grain.
2. **Chapter 2: The Dry Road** (`dry_farmland`, `ruined_village`, `mountain_road`, `army_camp_dusk`)
   - Myra guides Torrik past parched mills and Selva's ruined village. Nova warns of empty outriders on the mountain pass; at dusk, Rowan presents his pierced shield and unsigned delivery stubs.
3. **Chapter 3: Shadows in the Ledger** (`night_alley`, `archive_room`, `great_guild_headquarters`)
   - Nova steers Torrik through the night alley to Archivist Ilvar, who compares field numbers against vault duplicates and finds twelve diverted river barges before Fennick confronts them at headquarters.
4. **Chapter 4: Before the Council** (`council_chamber`, `small_guild_hall`)
   - Preliminary hearing in the Council Chamber. Fennick demands physical barge manifests and stalls the committee into a 48-hour recess while quietly ordering his factors to move the cargo upstream.
5. **Chapter 5: River Barges at Dawn** (`city_square`, `rainy_street`, `night_alley`)
   - Myra and Orsen track Fennick's lockhouse clerks through wet backstreets. Torrik decides whether to spare a coerced lockmaster in exchange for the private barge ledger.
6. **Chapter 6: The Forge's Oath** (`blacksmith_workshop`, `small_guild_hall`, `dry_farmland`)
   - Orsen's ward smiths repair wagon axles on cold iron; Haldor commits outer-ward escort riders to move emergency seed and tools across the dry valley.
7. **Chapter 7: The Barred Archive** (`archive_room`, `great_guild_headquarters`, `night_alley`)
   - Hired thugs try to burn the duplicate shelves in the Archive Room during the night watch. Ilvar, Nova, and Torrik bar the oak doors and rescue the stamped harbor registers.
8. **Chapter 8: Ridge of Smoke** (`mountain_road`, `ruined_village`, `army_camp_dusk`)
   - Climbing back toward the pass with relief carts, Torrik helps Selva organize shelter grain at the ruined village before learning Fennick's mercenaries are marching on the ridge camp.
9. **Chapter 9: Ash and Tally** (`army_camp_dusk`, `mountain_road`, `ruined_village`)
   - At the mountain tollhouse, Rowan's militia and Orsen's smiths intercept the diverted iron-and-grain convoy. Pure grit, barricaded carts, and written proof compel the caravan drivers to stand down.
10. **Chapter 10: The Open Ledger** (`great_guild_headquarters`, `council_chamber`, `city_square`, `small_guild_hall`)
    - Full assembly before the elders and ward witnesses. Accumulated flags (`trust_orsen`, `saved_receipts`, `allied_ilvar`, `spoke_for_selva`, `spared_lockmaster`, `protected_vault`, `shared_relief`) determine whether Fennick is stripped of office and the Great Guild charter is permanently reformed, or a hard-won popular audit takes root in the Outer Ward.
